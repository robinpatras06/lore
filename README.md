## 🤔 Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need Obsidian installed?
Yes, lore uses Obsidian to display your notes. Obsidian is free and available at [obsidian.md](https://obsidian.md).

### Will this slow down my computer?
No. lore is built with Rust, which is known for being extremely fast and lightweight. You won't even notice it running.

### Can I use lore with multiple AI tools?
Absolutely! That's one of lore's best features. You can switch between Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and others - lore keeps everything in one place.

### Is my data safe?
Yes. Everything is stored locally on your computer. No one else can access your notes, and you can back them up like any other file.

### What if I don't use Obsidian?
You can still use lore - it exports notes in standard markdown format, which works with many other note-taking apps.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### lore isn't saving sessions
- Make sure lore is running in the background
- Check that your AI tool is properly connected
- Restart both lore and your AI tool

### Notes aren't showing in Obsidian
- Verify the Obsidian vault path in lore settings
- Try refreshing Obsidian (Ctrl+R)
